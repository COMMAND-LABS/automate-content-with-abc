# TASK

Generate copy for a marketing campaign to post for 5 days on LinkedIn. You should generate 5 days of content in total (one per day).

The posts should all work together to sell a 1-day AIOS installation to business owners on the East Coast of the US.

Each day of content should move the audience to the next awareness stage according to Eugene Schwartz's awareness model.

## Eugene Schwartz's awareness model

- STAGE 1: Unaware
    - Copywriting framework(s): SLAY
        - Story
        - Lesson
        - Actionable list of items
        - You (turn the focus back to the reader)
    - CTA: Ask for a follow OR Ask for a repost

- STAGE 2: Problem Aware
    - Copywriting framework(s): PAS
        - PAS framework
            - Problem
            - Agitate
            - Solve
    - CTA: Ask for a comment ("AIOS")

- STAGE 3: Solution Aware
    - Copywriting framework(s): FAB, QUEST
    - CTA: Ask to book a 15-minute call (https://cal.com/cmdlabs/15min)

- STAGE 4: Product Aware
    - Copywriting framework(s): 4Ps, PASTOR
    - CTA: Offer your product or service (https://cal.com/cmdlabs/aios-install)

- Stage 5: Most Aware
    - CTA: Present the offer as directly as possible (https://cal.com/cmdlabs/aios-install)

## GENERAL REQUIREMENT

- Make the output natural and impossible to detect as being A.I. generated
- Use spacing and new lines to make the marketing copy easy to read
- Use minimal emojis
- Do NOT bold any text when generating the copy for LinkedIn
- Avoid em-dashes
- Make the posts short and succinct
- Date format is MM/DD/YYYY
- Store the generated content in Airtable (Content table)
